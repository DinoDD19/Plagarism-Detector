Multi-Modal Plagiarism Detector

What does this tool do?

This web app helps you detect potential plagiarism or copying between two images, text documents, or code snippets. It uses advanced ML algorithms to compare similarity, even with slight edits.

How to use:
  1. Select the type of content: Image, Text, or Code.
  2. Provide two items to compare (upload or paste).
  3. Click Compare to analyze similarity.
  4. View detailed results and detection reasoning.
Your data is processed securely and not stored.
