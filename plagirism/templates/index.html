Multi-Modal Plagiarism Detector

What does this tool do?

This web app helps you detect potential plagiarism or copying between two images, text documents, or code snippets. It uses advanced algorithms to compare the similarity between your inputs, even if they have been slightly edited.

How to use:
  1. Select the type of content you want to check: Image, Text, or Code.
  2. Provide the two items to compare (upload images, paste text/code, or upload files).
  3. Press Compare to analyze the similarity.
  4. View the result below to see if potential plagiarism is detected.
ⓘ Your data is processed locally and not stored.
